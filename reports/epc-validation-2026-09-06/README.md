# First EPC comparison

Open [the visual report](index.html). It contains the metrics, every selected
building, sources, assumptions, exclusions and 36 saved geometry views.

Twelve actual-year simulations and twelve paired TMY simulations completed.
Actual-year MAPE is 9.46%; total heat bias is −9.87%; intensity RMSE is
17.13 kWh/m²/year and intensity R² is −0.22. Ten buildings are within ±20%.

The target is EPC-reported delivered district heat for space heating plus hot
water. It is not primary energy, and individual-building raw meter accuracy is
not established. The six older records are allocated; all twelve outlines are
roof-edge proxies. This is a frozen, untuned exploratory pilot.
